Chemical return vehicle. Launches from lunar surface to intercept M-type NEAs, extracts semi-raw Ni-Fe/PGM ore, and returns to SPA IZ for docking and processing. ISRU-refuelled from P4+. 6–18 month round-trip missions. Phased down from P9 as Mk II takes over.

Heavy asteroid processor with on-asteroid beneficiation. Magnetic drum separator + centrifuge processes 500 t in-situ, returns 10 t of 50× PGM-concentrated material (~17.5 kg PGM/mission). Weeks-to-months dwell time at asteroid.

PROBE Mk III SEP Spacecraft — Ion P11+ (DG-11.6)
Ion-propelled bulk miner. NEVER lands on the Moon — stays in orbit permanently. Electromagnetic anchor pads engage ferromagnetic M-type surfaces; scraper mining 6–12 months; ejects 10 t Fe-Ni/PGM canisters from LLO on ballistic deorbit to EM catcher at SPA. Zero ISRU LH₂/LOX consumption. Build-out ~86/yr Y60–Y80.

MOLE-I PSR ice extraction rover
Permanently tethered rover at ~40 K on Shackleton PSR floor. Rotary-percussive drill, onboard mini-VEX sublimation chamber, 50 L water tank, dumps to substation for heated pipeline transfer to rim ISRU plant. Yields 5,692 kg propellant/yr per unit. The binding ISRU asset — fleet count determines every other fleet size. Demand-driven: max ~355 (P10), drops to ~50 at Y85+ as Mk III PROBE eliminates chemical propellant demand.

Builds roads (~100 m/day), pads, walls, docking collars, brick/tile. 50 m crew exclusion zone. Deploys to both SPA and PKT.

Mining-capable surface vehicle replacing both SKIP and MOLE-S at PKT. Na-S battery requires thermal management (270°C solidification → must never freeze). ~57% in-situ manufactured at P8, improving to ~80% post S-type.

Monitored-autonomy: monitors and recommends autonomously, requires human approval for irreversible actions.
