Full Programme Value Chain — P13+ Diversified Asteroids

M-type (Y75/Y85) · C-type (Y95/Y105) · S-type (Y115/Y125) · DRO processing · Earth independence by P14

M-type Asteroid

Redirect Y75 · Online Y85 (P12)

Products: Fe-Ni structural metal + PGM (Pt, Pd, Rh, Ir). 36.5 t/yr PGM from first capture. Ir for H₂ economy PEM electrolysers.

Processing at SPA: Magnetic separation → chlorination → selective precipitation. PGM to Earth via MD-4.

Enables: Fe-Ni for PKT foundry (hauler hoppers, conveyor pans, Earth-return canister shells). Bulk structural independence from Earth.

C-type Asteroid

Redirect Y95 · Online Y105 (P13)

Products: Water ice + CO₂ + organics. Propellant independence from PSR ice (MOLE-I →~50). MLI blankets preserve subsurface ice at DRO.

Processing at SPA: Pyrolysis 400–600°C → water electrolysis + Fischer-Tropsch (CO+H₂ → hydrocarbons).

Enables: Organic chemistry (polymers, reagent synthesis). Circuit Earth frac drops 10%→3%. Propellant self-sufficiency.

S-type Asteroid

Redirect Y115 · Online Y125 (P14)

Products: Silicon (semiconductor wafers) + Copper (motor windings, electronics). The final Earth dependency closure.

Processing at SPA: Czochralski crystal pulling (~1,700°C) for Si boules. Cu electrolytic refining to >99.9%. Products to PKT via MD-3.

Enables: In-situ control electronics + Cu wire for SRM motors. Circuit Earth frac →0.8%. Earth independence gate DG-14.6.

P14+ Steady State — Earth Independence

2,500,000 t/yr REO — full replacement of terrestrial REE mining. +$121.8B/yr net surplus from Y181. 182 Mt CO₂/yr avoided. Programme cost $21.3T; BCR 0.50 (200-yr) / 2.06 (annual steady state). Earth mass dependency <0.5% (avionics, specialty catalysts).

Each asteroid type enables the next: M-type Fe-Ni for C-type processing infrastructure → C-type organics for S-type chemistry → S-type Si/Cu for electronics independence. The Laser Ablation Truss at DRO (350 MW, 1,000 t/day, spiral surface ablation) processes captured asteroids continuously. Processed concentrate mass-driven DRO→SPA via MD-5.

Decommissioned MOLE-I Pu-238 RTGs stockpiled for deep-space missions. SPA becomes cislunar launch hub: propellant depot, mass driver network, low-gravity manufacturing. Programme infrastructure becomes permanent foundation for lunar-based space industry.
