Construction Phasing — Milestone & Event Definitions

Cross-reference to Construction Phasing Gantt and Master Timeline (MTL v8.5). All month numbers (M##) count from programme go-ahead (M1). Year = ceiling(M/12).

Cargo Launch Manifest (from MTL v8.5)

All hardware arrives via Starship-class vehicles. After the first landing at PLZ, all subsequent landings target ELZ sintered pads.
M20–23Cargo Launch 1 (first landing at PLZ): MOLE-S ×1, SINTER ×1, ARM-C ×1, SENTINEL ×1, initial solar arrays, MOLE-I ×4 (P3 batch), pre-fab PROBE capture collar ×2 (provisional, non-pressurised). PROBE-Scout ×3 launched M21 (one-way expendable NEA survey).
M42–45Cargo Launch 2 (first ELZ landing): FSP-1, additional solar arrays, ISRU equipment (VEX, electrolyser, cryo tanks), prefab hab pressure modules, SKIP propellant depot, MOLE-S ×1, SINTER ×1.
M62–65Cargo Launch 3: PROBE batch-2 (×3), spares batch-1, beneficiation module components, life support / hydroponics / medical bay prefab modules, SINTER ×1 (3rd unit).
M80–83Crew Launch: Crew-1 (4 persons) + co-manifested FSP-2 + P4 eclipse battery (250 kWh, 1 t).
M108–111Cargo Launch 4: PROBE batch-3 (×4), ops-phase robot fleet (ARM ×2, MOLE spares, SKIP ×1), DART ×1, SURVEY-2, solar expansion panels.
M138–141Crew Rotation Launch: Crew-2 (staggered swap P1→P4 at M136/139/142/145). 1–3 month overlap for knowledge transfer.
M154–157Cargo Launch 5: PROBE batch-4 (×5), spares batch-3, processing facility prefab batch-1.

Programme Milestones (from MTL v8.5)

Nine formally defined milestones gate programme progression. Each must be verified (by SENTINEL autonomously, by crew where present, or by Earth mission control) before subsequent activities are authorised.
MonthYearIDMilestone & Description
M20Y2M0Relay Network Live. 3× EML-2 halo orbit satellites validated. Continuous south pole comms coverage confirmed. Robot fleet launch cleared — without this, surface autonomy faces blackout windows that make fully autonomous operation unacceptably risky.
M36Y3M1First PROBE Landing. PROBE-Scout XRF data received from 3 expendable one-way NEA survey vehicles (launched M21). Shape models, spectral maps, and standoff XRF confirm first M-type asteroid targets. Scouts expended at NEAs (no physical return). First PROBE-Ops vehicles (×3) arrive from Earth at M24; collar docking begins M68. SURVEY continuously active in polar LLO since P2.
M54Y5M2First SINTER Print. SINTER-1 begins microwave sintering of hab shell Layer 0 (200 m² pad). Modular ring design confirmed — no permanent structures block future living module expansion arm. SENTINEL validates structural integrity of first in-situ manufactured component. Zero imported concrete.
M70Y6M4First SKIP Hop. SKIP-1 executes first sub-orbital hop to nearest KREEP-rich SPA Basin zone using ISRU-produced LH₂/LOX. Lands on dedicated SKIP pad (separate from PROBE collars). Returns with KREEP regolith sample. First use of lunar-produced propellant for surface transport.
M82Y7M3Crew-1 Arrival. 4 persons (Commander, Mission Scientist, Systems Engineer, Medical Officer). Pre-conditions verified: base pressurised, ISRU operational, SENTINEL structural clearance complete, 90-day consumables buffer present (1.9 t), crew return vehicle fuelled, PROBE fleet 4–6 active (docking station online M68), SKIP-1 hops since M70. ECLSS commissioning begins immediately (target 3–4 weeks, contingency 6–8 weeks). Crew vessel remains powered and habitable as fallback habitat ≥8 weeks.
M105Y9M5Beneficiation Online. Module commissioned. Double airlock dust containment verified. Physical concentration begins: crusher → screens → magnetic separation. KREEP concentrate 2–8× grade uplift. No acids required at this stage.
M116Y10M6First Export Batch. First combined export: semi-raw asteroid Ni-Fe/PGM + KREEP REE concentrate dispatched to Earth orbit depot. Planetary protection screening complete. Two independent revenue streams active. PROBE fleet at 10, SKIP fleet at 2.
M130Y11M8First DART PKT Mission. DART-1 ascends to LLO (~1,800 m/s), coasts to PKT zone, performs powered deorbit + autonomous landing at unimproved site. Onboard XRF confirms PKT REE grade. Ballistic arc physically ruled out at 3,300 km range — LLO orbital transfer is the only viable architecture. Returns with ~1,200 kg payload. PKT thorium/REE grade 5–10× SPA Basin average validates the fuel investment for the autonomous second base.
M136Y11.5M7Crew Rotation Begins. Staggered swap: P1 descends M136, P2 M139, P3 M142, P4 M145. Timed to cargo launches. 1–3 month knowledge-transfer overlap. Crew-2 composition may include processing specialist if P7 facility decision approved.

Construction Phase Events (Month-by-Month Reference)

Events referenced in the Construction Phasing Gantt, mapped to programme months. These are engineering events, not formal milestones — they track the physical construction sequence.
MonthYearStageEvent
M1–20Y1–2Earth-side: EML-2 relay constellation design, build, launch, and validation. Robot fleet fabrication and integration testing. All hardware Earth-side.
M21–28Y2Cargo launch campaign: Multiple Starship cargos dispatched. PROBE-Scouts launched M21 (expendable, one-way). Robot fleet transit to Moon.
M24Y2First cargo landing at PLZ. First and only landing on ridge crest. SINTER, MOLE-S, ARM-C, SENTINEL, solar panels offloaded. PLZ becomes Power Zone permanently — no further landings here (ejecta protection).
M24–25Y2PLZ charging pad sintered + solar deployed. MOLE-S levels ~200 m². SINTER sinters surface. ARM-C deploys initial solar panel frames. First permanent off-grid charging point established. (~14 days)
M24–25Y2MOLE-S pre-grades ELZ site (concurrent). Blade levels 80 m circle at ELZ location, ~4.3 km from PLZ. ~7–10 days.
M25Y2–3SINTER drives to ELZ. Self-powered (onboard reactor). 4.3 km traverse on MOLE-S pre-graded terrain. ~12 hours.
M25–28Y3ELZ Pad 1 centre zone sintered. SINTER sinters 20 m diameter centre patch (1–2 passes, ~50 mm depth) on pre-graded 80 m circle. Sufficient for Starship landing legs (9 m footprint). ~40 days. (Option C: light-sintered centre for early landings.)
~M28Y3ELZ declared landable. Centre zone sintered. Cargo lander #2 can target ELZ Pad 1.
M28–60+Y3–5ELZ Pad 1 full-depth sintering (background). Expanding outward to 80 m total diameter, 360 mm depth (6 passes), 270° blast berm. ~48,000 SINTER-hr. Largest single construction task in the programme. Ongoing through end of P3.
M26–28Y3ARM-C drops interim charging waypoint at HAB zone. Battery pack + small solar panel set at HAB-zone midpoint (~1.5 km from PLZ). Allows ARM-C mid-route charging during long ELZ↔PLZ hauls.
M25–34Y3Road grading PLZ→HAB→ELZ. MOLE-S blade grades ~4.3 km route. Graded but unsintered initially — road sintering is lowest SINTER priority. ARM-C operates on graded surface at reduced speed (~0.05 m/s).
M28–32Y3Cargo landers #2–3 at ELZ. FSP-1, solar array expansion panels, cable spools, ISRU equipment, hab modules, MOLE-S #2, SINTER #2, additional MOLE-I units. All subsequent programme cargo lands here.
M30–34Y3FSP-1 haul ELZ→PLZ. ARM-C + heavy trailer. 6,600 kg, 4.3 km uphill. ~50+ hours loaded (charging stops required). The programme's hardest single transport operation — becomes the standard procedure for all 5 FSP deliveries through P7.
M30–40Y3±2,800 VDC trunk cable PLZ→ISRU. SINTER builds sintered trench (400 × 300 mm). ARM-C lays OFHC Cu cable. DC-DC converter at ISRU entry steps down to 1,000 VDC internal bus.
M30–35Y31,000 VDC cable PLZ→HAB. 3 km downhill in sintered trench. DC-DC converter at HAB sub-floor steps to 120 VDC module bus.
M36Y3Power hub commissioned. Solar string combiners + FSP-1 input breakers + bus-tie switches + SENTINEL power management interface. Base grid live. All zones can draw power.
M36–41Y3–4±2,800 VDC trunk PLZ→IZ. ~1.8 km. Lower priority than ISRU and HAB branches. IZ converter steps to 1,000 VDC gantry bus.
M32–40Y3–4ISRU plant installation. ARM-C hauls VEX, electrolyser, cryo tanks from ELZ. SINTER builds foundations and enclosures. ±2,800 VDC energised. 1,000 VDC internal distribution commissioned.
M34–42Y3–4Heated pipeline ISRU→CAP. 3 km. SS tube (80 bar, 20 mm OD) co-routed with 1,000 VDC PSR trunk cable in sintered trench. Trace heating at 7 W/m.
M40–43Y4PSR trunk cable descent. Cable + pipeline lowered from CAP rim anchor 8.2 km down crater wall via winch at 0.1 m/s (~23 hr). Pre-integrated SATS+WEB assembly at cable end arrives at CLP already powered and operational (pre-heated to 293 K, 3× LWRHU maintain ≥268 K during descent).
M43Y4CLP powered. First powered node on PSR floor. GATE 2 in autonomous PSR deployment sequence.
M43–55Y4–5MOLE-I fleet descent + hopscotch build-out. First MOLE-I descends (4.7 hr on transit battery). Plugs into CLP for grid power. Pulls first 500 m spine cable to Junction 1 (2.1 km from CLP). J1 live. Hop-scotch continues: plug into junction, work within 100 m radius, hop to next. Branch build-out: ~3–5 days per branch (200 posts, 3 km) per MOLE-I. 10 units deployed by P3 end across 2 substations.
M43–53Y4–5Substation deployment. Pre-assembled on PTFE-runner sleds (~160 kg). Lowered to CLP staging area. Sled clips to spine cable; MOLE-I pushes along spine to target junction, transfers to branch, pushes to final position. Anchors deploy, riser connected. WEB powers up. ~48 hr from rim dispatch to first water per new substation.
~M46Y4First water production. MOLE-I drills icy permafrost, sublimates water in mini-VEX, dumps to substation, water flows via heated pipeline to ISRU plant. Electrolyser commissioned. H₂/O₂ production begins. Propellant stockpile accumulates. Programme transitions from 100% Earth propellant toward ISRU self-sufficiency.
M38–43Y4Hab cradle excavation. MOLE-S excavates ~1,300 t of regolith to create the sunken cradle profile for habitat module placement.
M40–54Y4–5Berm construction. MOLE-S blade pushes regolith fill. SINTER follows with shell sintering. 2.5 m overburden for passive radiation shielding (zero energy input, zero import). Multi-month sequential lift-and-sinter process.
M52–56Y5SINTER hab shell Layer 0 (= Milestone M2). First in-situ manufactured structural component sintered directly onto berm. SENTINEL validates.
M47–55Y4–5Pressure module placement. ARM-C positions H1, H2, Ops modules into cradle from ELZ (~1.3 km haul each). CBM berthing interfaces connected.
M52–56Y5O₂ + water lines from ISRU to HAB. 0.4 km. Life-critical. O₂ line: dome-loaded regulator at ISRU end, double-block-and-bleed at hab entry. Pressure-tested. Water line: remineralisation + UV sterilisation at hab end.
M55–60Y5ECLSS racks installed (not commissioned). Sabatier reactor, OGA electrolyser, CDRA CO₂ removal. Positioned by ARM-C. Commissioning is a crew task (323 crew-hours on arrival).
M60–63Y5SENTINEL structural validation. Full exterior + interior scan of hab complex. Pressure hold test. Thermal verification. SENTINEL clearance is a hard pre-crew gate.
M63Y5–6Hab declared structurally clear. SENTINEL sign-off. Hab is pressure-tight, radiation-shielded, powered, O₂/water-connected, ECLSS-installed. Awaiting crew for ECLSS commissioning.
M32Y3Provisional docking collar deployed. Cargo Launch 1 hardware. Non-pressurised. Surface ARM transfer only. For PROBE-Scout data receipt and initial PROBE-Ops staging.
M50–58Y4–5IZ pad sintering. SINTER builds PROBE and SKIP landing pads + gantry column foundations at IZ campus.
M55–63Y5Permanent docking collars × 2. SINTER builds pressurised cylinders (3,000 mm ID × 4,500 mm, sintered regolith walls 500–1,000 mm, Al 6061 pressure liner). Replaces provisional collar.
M60–65Y5–6Gantry rail + ARM-D installation. Imported steel I-beam rails on sintered columns. ARM-D ×2 mounted. 1,000 VDC bus bar along rail. 6-tool carousel per ARM-D.
M58–65Y5–6Propellant farm + cryo transfer lines. ISRU→IZ 0.7 km. LH₂ + LOX vacuum-jacketed lines. 4× isolation valve stations. SENTINEL monitors at 10 Hz (H₂ leak, pressure, UV flame). Auto-close on threshold exceedance.
M68Y6Docking station declared operational. 2 permanent pressurised collars + 2 ARM-D + propellant feed + SENTINEL oversight. Full 48–72 hr PROBE turnaround cycling begins.
M65–72Y6CRV positioned + fuelled. Crew return vehicle on dedicated hardstand. Propellant from ISRU. Maintained fuelled at all times. Non-negotiable pre-crew safety gate.
M68–75Y690-day consumables buffer loaded. 1.9 t total (324 kg O₂ + 900 kg H₂O + 648 kg food + 4 kg N₂). MATLAB-verified. Stored aboard crew vessel (fallback habitat).
M70Y6First SKIP hop (= Milestone M4). SKIP-1 sub-orbital hop using ISRU propellant. KREEP sample return. Crew assumes supervisory oversight of hop cadence on arrival.
M82Y7Crew-1 arrival (= Milestone M3). 4 persons. All pre-crew gates verified. ECLSS commissioning begins (323 crew-hours, 3–4 weeks baseline). The robots built the base; the crew makes it habitable.
