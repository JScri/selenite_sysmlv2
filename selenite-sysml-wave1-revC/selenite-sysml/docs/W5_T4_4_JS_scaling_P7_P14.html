Asteroid Resources Online

M-type (Y85): PGM + Fe-Ni structural metal. C-type (Y105): water ice + organics → propellant independence. MLI blankets preserve subsurface ice. S-type (Y125): Si + Cu → electronics independence. Each asteroid type enables the next: M-type Fe-Ni for C-type processing infrastructure; C-type organics for S-type chemistry. Laser Ablation Truss at DRO for captured asteroids.

Beyond REE Displacement

LOX by-product economy: KREEP processing generates surplus LOX — foundation for cislunar propellant market. Deep-space staging: lunar propellant depot + mass driver network as departure point for Mars+ missions. In-situ reagent synthesis long-term goal. Stockpiled Pu-238 RTGs from decommissioned MOLE-I for deep-space probes. Solar power satellites from lunar Al + Si. Programme infrastructure becomes permanent cislunar industrial foundation.
