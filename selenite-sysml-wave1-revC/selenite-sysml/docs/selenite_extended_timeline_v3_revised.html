Steady State: Programme transitions from “betterment of Earth” to “self-sustaining cislunar industrial civilisation.” 2.5 Mt/yr REO fully replaces terrestrial REE mining. 182 Mt CO₂/yr avoided (France + UK combined). M/C/S-type asteroid resources provide near-complete material independence. Infrastructure supports indefinite operation and growth without Earth resupply. PKT resource adequacy: ~220–660 Mt REE accessible to 200 m depth (100–300 ppm bulk grade), providing ~88–264 years of supply at 2.5 Mt/yr from Y180. Post-P14 expansion: hauler upgrades (deeper mining attachments, relay depot chains), fleet expansion across PKT’s 6.1M km², or new extraction methods for depths beyond 200 m. Terrestrial REE recycling (projected 90%+ by 23rd century) progressively reduces demand. Resource depletion is not a binding constraint on any programme-relevant timescale.
