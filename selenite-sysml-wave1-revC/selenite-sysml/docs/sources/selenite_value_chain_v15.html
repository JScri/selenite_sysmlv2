Dual-Site Split Value Chain (ECN-019 Rev C)

SPA Shackleton proves KREEP chemistry (5 circuits, P6–P9) and serves as the permanent asteroid processing hub. All M-type PGM hydromet, C-type pyrolysis, and S-type Czochralski processing occurs at SPA. PROBE fleet (Mk I/II/III) bases permanently at SPA. Mk III canisters arrive via EM catcher (never dock at IZ).

PKT Procellarum handles all production-scale KREEP processing from P8 onward. Zero permanent crew. ~2.08M optimised circuits at P14 steady state (1.2 t/yr, 400 kW each). Hauler Rev B for mining (1.5M at P14), steel apron conveyors for Tier 3 transport (~87,500 km). Thorium MSR power from KREEP waste (ThCl₄).

5 mass drivers link the nodes electromagnetically: MD-1 SPA→PKT (propellant/reagent), MD-2 PKT→Earth (REO), MD-3 PKT→SPA (ThCl₄ for SPA MSR), MD-4 SPA→Earth (PGM), MD-5 DRO→SPA (asteroid concentrate). Total YBCO: ~303 t lifetime.

8-step zero-carbon flowsheet: D2EHPA eliminated. Group separation on Moon (LREE via (NH₄)₂SO₄, HREE via H₃PO₄). Individual SX on Earth. In-situ H₂SO₄ from troilite, Ca(OH)₂ from quickite. Earth reagent: ~750 t/yr (was ~6.9 Mt/yr pre-ECN-019).
