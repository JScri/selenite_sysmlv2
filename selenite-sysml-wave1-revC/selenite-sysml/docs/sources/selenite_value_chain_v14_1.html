Selenite Programme · Value Chain v14.1

Earth – Moon – Asteroid Value Chain

SPA Shackleton only · P0–P7 Foundation Era · Single-site architecture · HØW competition scope

Phase 1 — Foundation

P0–P3 · Y1–Y7

Flow is entirely one-directional (Earth → Moon). Earth exports all initial capital: robot fleet, pressure modules, solar arrays, ISRU equipment, FSP reactors. SPA has no independent revenue. Autonomous construction by SINTER, MOLE-S, ARM. SENTINEL supervises from Earth (2.56 s RTT).

Phase 2 — Production

P4–P6 · Y7–Y15

ISRU reaches self-sufficiency — PROBE refuels from lunar propellant, not Earth supply. 30 PROBEs intercept M-type NEAs, returning 1,500 kg Ni-Fe/PGM per mission. SKIP collects SPA Basin KREEP (274 hops/yr/unit). Flow becomes bi-directional (Moon ↔ Asteroids). Beneficiation module online. First export batch (M116, Y10).

Phase 3 — Scale

P7 · Y18–Y25

Processing facility proves 8-step zero-carbon flowsheet (D2EHPA eliminated). ~0.4 t/yr REO proof-of-concept. REE group concentrates + PGM dispatched to Earth via Starship. DART confirms PKT site. Progressive automation validates PKT zero-crew architecture. YBCO delivery begins for MD-1. This is the proving ground — every system that must work at PKT is tested here first.

★ The gravity-well advantage: Launching from Moon (1.87 km/s to LLO) vs Earth (9.4 km/s to LEO) transforms asteroid mining from prohibitively expensive one-off missions into a fleet operation with diminishing marginal costs. The stockpile-and-release model (refining on Moon, controlled dispatch to Earth) prevents market saturation — the failure mode that undermines every Earth-direct asteroid mining proposal.

★ What comes next: P7 is the HØW competition target and the programme's proof-of-concept milestone. Beyond P7, the dual-site architecture activates: PKT takes over KREEP production at industrial scale (see selenite_value_chain_v15.html), and diversified asteroid captures extend the programme to PGM displacement and Earth independence (see selenite_value_chain_v16.html).
