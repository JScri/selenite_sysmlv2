    Returns PGM concentrate only, not raw ore. 50x mass reduction.
    Required for PGM scaling beyond 5 t/yr.
    Full 2100 PGM demand (8,000 t/yr) requires orbital processing
    platforms + asteroid capture/redirect — P13+ infrastructure.

  MOLE-I Mk II (P8+): Extended drill for deep ice (10m+ strip mining).
    Retractable drill assembly (must fully retract for transit).
    Options: (a) Mk II new-build with telescoping drill, or
    (b) Mk I retrofit: replace drill module at existing substations.
    Design consideration: drill retraction clearance vs chassis height.
    If retrofit: drill module is an ORU — ARM swaps at substation.

    5 t payload, electric drive, 1 m/s on sintered road.

    Spec document needed: SEL-HAULER-001 Rev A.
