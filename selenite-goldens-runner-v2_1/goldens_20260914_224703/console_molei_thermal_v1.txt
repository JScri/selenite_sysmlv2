================================================================
  THERMAL VERIFICATION — CONCLUSIONS
================================================================
  1. Original 57W WEB heater was never derived from bottom-up
     thermal model. Even unoptimised baseline is ~15.2W.
  2. Optimised WEB (PEEK standoffs, Mn cables, PEEK pipe couplers)
     reduces total heat loss to ~3.2W at 273K / 40K ambient.
  3. Structural mounts (Path A) dominate the optimised budget.
     Standoff geometry matters: large cross-section or thin
     washers can make PEEK isolation WORSE than bare Ti bolts.
  4. Thermal mass (223 kJ/K from 50 kg water) is enormous:
     even with ZERO LWRHUs, WEB takes >200 hr to reach 253K.
  5. LWRHU SIZING:
     Minimum for 250K (floor+wall): 3 LWRHUs (3.3W)
     T_eq (PSR floor, 40K): 278K  [PASS >250K]
     T_eq (wall transit, 25K): 263K  [PASS >250K]
     Mass: 126g (3×42g)  PuO₂: 8.0g
  6. T_eq=278K is ABOVE setpoint (273K) — thermostat-controlled
     heater is REQUIRED to prevent overheating in steady state.
     Any count ≥4 LWRHU pushes T_eq well above safe limits.
  7. Electric heater re-sized: 57W → ~5W (transient control only)
  8. CAUTION: k(T) polynomial fits, MLI degradation factor,
     and contact resistances all require measured validation.
     Sensitivity analysis (Section 9) bounds the uncertainty.
