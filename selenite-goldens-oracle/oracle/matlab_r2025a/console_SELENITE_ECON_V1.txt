================================================================
  KEY FINDINGS
================================================================

  1. The programme is commercially NEGATIVE at all discount rates
     above ~0.0% due to the 40-60 year lag between investment
     and significant revenue.

  2. INCLUDING monetised environmental externalities, the
     programme achieves positive NPV at discount rates ≤0.0%,
     consistent with Arrow et al. (2014) declining schedule.

  3. The P8-P9 chemical transport era is the cost bottleneck:
     reagent delivery alone requires ~1175 Starship flights/yr
     at P9. In-situ reagent production is economically essential.

  4. Net environmental benefit (avoided damage minus launch
     carbon) is $1257.6B — launch pollution is dwarfed by
     avoided terrestrial mining damage by >3×.

  5. The programme is best justified as a sovereign/consortium
     investment capturing intergenerational public goods,
     analogous to nuclear waste management or climate adaptation.
