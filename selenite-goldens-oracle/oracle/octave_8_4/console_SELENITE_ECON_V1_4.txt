INFRASTRUCTURE DERIVATION (bottom-up):
  REO target (exogenous, programme plan)
  → Circuits needed = ceil(REO / 1.2 t/yr per circuit)
  → Regolith throughput = REO / 500 ppm
  → Haulers needed = regolith × (1 - Tier3 frac) / 1500 t/yr per hauler
  → Conveyor km = regolith × Tier3 frac / 20,000 t/yr per km
  → Power (kW) = circuits × 400 kW each
  → MSR count = power / 100 MWe per MSR (from P9+)
  → MOLE-I = propellant demand / 5692 kg/yr per unit
  → Canisters/yr = REO / 3.5 t per canister (from P9+)

EARTH CARGO DERIVATION:
  For each infrastructure type:
    New-build cargo = delta(fleet) × unit mass × Earth fraction
    Maintenance cargo = fleet × unit mass × maint rate × Earth fraction
  Earth fractions decline over time:
    Circuits: 100% (P8) → 10% (P10+) → 3% (C-type, Y105) → 0.8% (S-type, Y125)
    Haulers: 100% (P8) → 43% (P10+) → 33% (M-type Fe-Ni, Y90) → 20% floor
    MSR: 100% (Hastelloy-N) → 30% (Ni-201 in-situ, Y80+)
    Conveyors: 5% constant (drive electronics + bearings)

COST MODEL:
  Earth launch: cargo × launch cost × contingency
    Launch cost: $6,000/kg (P0-P7), $4,000 (P8-P9), $2,000 (P10+)
    Contingency: 50% (P0-P7), 35% (P8-P9), 25% (P10+)
  In-situ fabrication: insitu mass × $100/kg
  Mk III PROBEs: LEO launch only at $500/kg × contingency
  Redirect tugs: LEO launch only at $500/kg × contingency
  Asteroid processing: $500M/yr (C-type) + $800M/yr (S-type)
  R&D: $500M-$2,000M/yr scaling with programme maturity

REVENUE MODEL:
